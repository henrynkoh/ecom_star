# Ecom Star REA + Six Sigma Quality System

## Mission

Apply Reverse Engineering Agent methods and Six Sigma management to create original, measurable, repeatable, and compliant Amazon affiliate videos. The system studies successful public content for general communication principles, never to reproduce protected creative expression.

## Quality objective

Each published video must help a viewer make a better product decision while meeting all factual, media-rights, affiliate-disclosure, safety, and platform requirements.

The production unit is **one daily product-video run**.

## Defect definition

A defect is any condition that makes a run inaccurate, unsupported, unsafe, noncompliant, confusing, unreproducible, or improperly published.

### Critical defects: zero tolerance

- False or misleading ranking claim
- Unsupported material product claim
- Unlicensed or undocumented media use
- Missing Amazon affiliate disclosure
- Wrong affiliate tag or destination
- Wrong YouTube channel or visibility
- Active recall or serious safety issue not addressed
- Copyright block or known platform violation
- Credential, token, cookie, or personal-data exposure

### Major defects

- Missing buyer-fit, compatibility, limitation, or safety information
- Contradictory facts between sources
- Product shown does not match the linked ASIN
- Captions materially differ from narration
- Call to action appears before necessary limitations or disclosure
- Benchmark-derived creative is too similar to the source

### Minor defects

- Typographical error that does not change meaning
- Small caption timing issue
- Nonessential formatting inconsistency
- Missing optional performance metric

## REA evidence discipline

Every finding must carry:

- A finding ID
- Source URL or file path
- Observation timestamp
- Direct observation
- Interpretation or hypothesis
- Confidence: confirmed, probable, possible, or unknown
- Verification method
- Contradictory evidence, if any
- Decision and reviewer

Facts and hypotheses must never be mixed. A hypothesis becomes confirmed only when supported by an authoritative source, a reproducible observation, or independent verification appropriate to the claim.

## DMAIC workflow

### 1. Define

Create a run charter before research:

- Selected product, actual category, ASIN, and observed ranking context
- Target viewer and practical decision the video will help make
- Authorized sources and media-use boundaries
- Required safety, compatibility, and disclosure language
- Expected duration, format, and single primary call to action
- Stop conditions and human-approval conditions

Voice of Customer inputs may include public comments and search questions, but they are evidence of audience questions, not evidence of product facts.

### 2. Measure

Record a baseline before writing the script:

- Number of objective factual claims
- Number supported by primary or authoritative sources
- Number of media assets and documented usage bases
- Number of unresolved contradictions
- Script duration and word count
- Number of distinct visuals
- Number of buyer questions answered
- Rework cycles
- Production time

Required calculated measures:

- **Evidence coverage:** supported objective claims / total objective claims
- **Media-rights coverage:** assets with documented permission / assets used
- **Caption accuracy:** correctly represented spoken claims / spoken claims checked
- **First-pass yield:** eligible runs published without correction / eligible runs
- **Defect escape rate:** defects discovered after publication / published runs
- **Voice of Customer coverage:** priority buyer questions answered / priority questions selected

Never invent unavailable analytics. Mark a measurement unavailable and explain why.

### 3. Analyze

Identify why defects or weak performance occur using:

- Five Whys for each critical or repeated defect
- Pareto review of the defect categories causing most rework
- Fishbone review across source data, tools, process, platform, creative, and human review
- Source-conflict analysis
- Audience-question clustering
- Failure Mode and Effects Analysis

Separate correlation from causation. A successful public benchmark provides hypotheses about communication, not proof that one specific creative element caused its performance.

### 4. Improve

Use controlled improvements:

- Change one major creative variable at a time when practical
- Put the viewer, product identity, and useful outcome in the opening
- Order verified features by buyer decision value
- Show evidence or original diagrams for factual claims
- Put compatibility, limitations, safety, and disclosure before the final call to action
- Use one primary call to action
- Remove unsupported adjectives and earnings language
- Require an independent verifier to challenge the script and source manifest
- Preserve a private draft rather than publishing a failed quality gate

Benchmark learnings must be transformed, not copied. Use different wording, composition, pacing, colors, typography, footage, diagrams, narration, and scene order appropriate to the selected product.

### 5. Control

After publication:

- Re-open the public video and verify playback, title, visibility, description, disclosure, affiliate URL, and product match
- Log defects and corrections
- Compare the run with the previous 30 runs
- Review available retention, click, comment, and conversion signals without inventing missing data
- Classify public questions for the next Voice of Customer cycle
- Run a weekly Pareto review
- Run a monthly process-capability review
- Revalidate policy-sensitive steps whenever Amazon or YouTube changes its rules

## Agent roles

One agent may perform multiple roles, but each role must produce its own evidence:

1. **Scout:** finds the selected product and captures ranking evidence.
2. **REA Analyst:** inventories facts, product behavior, buyer questions, and benchmark principles.
3. **Source Verifier:** checks facts, recalls, safety, compatibility, and contradictions.
4. **Media-Rights Controller:** approves or rejects every visual and audio asset.
5. **Creative Designer:** writes an original product-specific treatment.
6. **Independent Challenger:** attempts to disprove claims and identify copied or confusing elements.
7. **Publisher:** verifies channel, settings, disclosure, affiliate link, and public playback.
8. **Control Monitor:** tracks defects, Voice of Customer signals, and improvement actions.

The Creative Designer cannot approve its own final factual and rights checks.

## Quality gates

### Gate A: selection

Pass only when ranking context, category, ASIN, timestamp, and source evidence are recorded.

### Gate B: eligibility

Pass only when availability, recall/safety review, legal/platform suitability, and affiliate destination are acceptable.

### Gate C: evidence

Pass only when:

- Evidence coverage for objective published claims is 100%
- No material contradiction remains unresolved
- Product identity and linked ASIN match

### Gate D: media rights

Pass only when media-rights coverage is 100%. An unclear asset is rejected, not treated as probably acceptable.

### Gate E: creative independence

Pass only when the video uses original wording and an independently designed visual treatment. Benchmark material may support principles but must not be recreated.

### Gate F: pre-publication

Pass only when:

- Required affiliate disclosure is present
- Relevant safety and compatibility caveats are present
- Captions are accurate
- Paid promotion and synthetic-content questions are answered truthfully
- Audience and visibility settings are correct
- Copyright checks show no blocking issue
- The signed-in channel is correct

### Gate G: post-publication

Pass only after the public page is reopened and playback, metadata, disclosure, link, and visibility are confirmed.

Any failed critical gate produces a blocked run. Do not substitute another product when the daily selection rule requires exactly one product.

## FMEA starter register

Score Severity, Occurrence, and Detection from 1 to 5. Risk Priority Number equals Severity times Occurrence times Detection. Any critical defect is blocking regardless of score.

- False overall-number-one wording: control with required category and timestamp language.
- Product and ASIN mismatch: control with a two-source identity check and final link test.
- Unsupported feature claim: control with claim-level evidence mapping.
- Unlicensed product image: control with an asset manifest and 100% rights gate.
- Missing disclosure: control with exact-text validation before upload.
- Stale price: control with an observed-time qualifier and final listing check.
- Unsafe use implication: control with product-specific limitation and safety review.
- Creative copying: control with an independent similarity challenge.
- Wrong channel or visibility: control with pre-upload and post-publication verification.
- Broken affiliate tag: control with URL parsing and destination confirmation.

## Control targets

- Critical defects before publication: 0
- Critical defects escaping publication: 0
- Objective-claim evidence coverage: 100%
- Media-rights coverage: 100%
- Required disclosure compliance: 100%
- Correct product-to-ASIN match: 100%
- Post-publication verification: 100%
- Unresolved material contradictions: 0

Performance metrics such as retention, clicks, and conversion are improvement signals. They never override factual, rights, disclosure, safety, or platform gates.

## Daily quality record

Each run log must include:

- Run charter and selected Voice of Customer questions
- Product/category/ASIN/rank evidence
- Claim-to-source matrix
- Recall, safety, and compatibility results
- Asset manifest and usage basis
- Script and scene plan
- Independent challenge findings
- Gate results and defects
- Rendered-file path
- YouTube ID and public URL
- Available performance measures
- Corrective and preventive actions
